# Notices and credits

**Modular Golems: Arcane Materials** is an unofficial, community-made addon. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
authors of Modular Golems, Ars Nouveau, Ars Elemental or Ars Elemancy.

## Licence
The original code and data are licensed under the **GNU LGPL v2.1** (see `LICENSE`), matching Modular Golems 3.x.
The addon bundles none of the mods it depends on; it only calls their public classes and references their items by id.

## Textures
The golem textures were generated by recolouring and re-laying-out the copper golem textures of Modular Golems 3.x
(LGPL-2.1, credit to the Modular Golems project); the colour schemes were designed after the armour sets of Ars Elemental
and Ars Elemancy. Construct icons are original.
