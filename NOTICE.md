The addon bundles none of the mods it depends on; it only calls their public classes and references their items by id.

## Textures
All textures are generated from scratch by the scripts in `tools/` (colours, zones and patterns are defined in code).
No texture files from Modular Golems or any other mod are used or included. The golem textures follow the standard
vanilla-style box layouts (iron golem, player and wolf models) that Modular Golems' golems use, so that they fit those models.
The colour schemes were designed after the armour sets of Ars Elemental and Ars Elemancy.
